The VÖRM Philosophy

Form
follows
frequency

VÖRM was founded on a single axiom: that an object capable of delivering transcendent sound must itself be transcendent in form. Not decoration placed upon function — but form and function fused at the molecular level, indistinguishable from one another.

The Étude No. 1 begins with a 50mm dynamic driver wound with 0.025mm copper wire, hand-tensioned to within microns of tolerance. Its titanium housing is milled from a single billet, then finished over eleven hours by a single craftsperson in our Copenhagen atelier.

We produce forty-seven pieces per year. Not because we can't produce more, but because each one deserves the time it requires to be what it is.
